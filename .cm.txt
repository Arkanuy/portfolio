PUNCH: portfolio as a punch-card deck and its reader

The old direction is gone. Component galleries on the web all repeat the same
things - shader buttons, liquid metal, 3D cloth, sylva/temple scenes, holo
cards, typography vortex, portal fields, constellation backgrounds. All of that
is deliberately avoided here: no WebGL, no shaders, no glass, no glow.

Instead: Hollerith 80-column cards. Each work record is one card and its HOLES
ARE ITS DATA. lib/punch.ts maps the data to card geometry with rules anyone can
check: column 1 stores the status zone, 2-5 the year, 6-7 how many parts were
built, 8-13 the first six letters of the title, 14-79 a check pattern whose
DENSITY follows the title length, column 80 an end mark.

The reader head sweeps columns as you scroll and the column it crosses lights
up. Picking a card LIFTS IT OUT OF THE DECK, and the print area below the
machine shows that card - including a legend so every hole can be accounted
for. Keys 1-7 pick a card.

Two defects measurement caught:
1. Every card had exactly 86 holes. The check pattern started as one hole per
   column, so density was identical and the cards read as decoration rather
   than data. Title length now drives the density; measured 86 / 119 / 152.
2. The accent failed contrast in the print area (3.28:1 light, 4.49:1 dark
   against the metal). Added a --metal-accent token with per-theme values
   (#7a1a0a 5.28:1 and #ff8a6e 5.35:1).

41 gates green, all 8 anti-slop gates at zero across 17 routes. Without JS all
833 holes still render and every step stays in the document.
