verify-kage: make the mobile and live checks actually work

Three instrument faults, all mine, found running against the live origin:

1. networkidle never settles - the hero scene animates continuously, so the
   network never goes idle and navigation timed out on several routes. Now
   domcontentloaded plus an explicit wait for the scene iframe.
2. One mobile navigation timed out on a cold start. Added a single retry;
   one repeat is more honest than an unbounded timeout.
3. The touch check now dispatches a staged touch drag instead of a mouse drag,
   and asserts a real distance rather than any movement.
