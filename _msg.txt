               The page is the scene and a credit line, nothing else.
  homepage     Scene block back to its original wording ("Kage - jalan malam
               lima bab melewati kuil gunung di Kyoto", "Buka scene").
  footer       Back to "Kage - ThreeUI scene".

  globals.css  The whole kageNight block and the .knight* styles deleted - the
               dark-theme remap and the two-page shell are pointless now that
               the whole site speaks the same visual language. /kage inherits
               the site chrome with no exceptions.

What STAYS is the style and the concept, which is what was actually asked for:
Onest from Kage's own font file, the Kage palette, sharp corners, hairline
chapter rules with automatic numbering, calmer headings, dark by default.

Two fixes along the way:

1. The scene was 1220x900 instead of 1440x900 - I had wrapped it in the content
   column. It is a full-bleed document; the credit line is the only part that
   belongs in the column. Now full width again.
2. .glass, used by the homepage scene block, was never defined in the CSS, so
   that panel had no edge at all. Defined now in the Kage language: 1px hairline,
   sharp corners, no blur, no shadow.

Three new gates so this cannot drift back: the scene stays full-bleed, no
invented Kage wording appears anywhere, and the homepage hero keeps its own
sentence. 29 gates green, plus the live origin.
